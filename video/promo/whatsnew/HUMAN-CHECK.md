# Promo C «Τι νέο» — what a human should check

1. Listen once at phone volume: the music splice at 26.8 s (on a half-bar of the track, measured, not heard) and the
   pin pops at 0.0 s and ~3.4 s.
2. App issue seen while filming (not fixed here; main was not touched): after a **map tap** on a stop, the map
   eases while the card still slides in, so the stop often ends at the card's top edge and the pin is half hidden
   (probably MapView.svelte `sheetPad()` reading `card.top` while the card slides in; not verified). The deep link places it well. Worth an issue on web.
   Still 1 is therefore taken from the deep link, not a tap.
3. The line card's «Οχήματα τώρα» list stayed «Φόρτωση…» in the paused-clock capture; the film shows that card for
   ~1 s only. Check it loads on a real phone.
4. Data is live from Sat 31/10 ~05:40 (dawn): few buses, so the stop card has 4–5 lines and the trains map is sparse.
   A weekday-morning recapture (same commands, README «Promo C») would look fuller.
5. Telegram and the weekly repeat are not in the film (the rail sheet offers browser push only); they are in
   still 4 (bus stop sheet with «Στο Telegram, κάθε εβδομάδα»). Feedback (v0.2.1) is not in the film either.
6. «Προαστιακός: ζωντανά» is not on screen: the suburban card mixes live times with a «πρόγραμμα» note, and a caption
   saying «ζωντανά» read as a contradiction. Say it in the thread text instead.
